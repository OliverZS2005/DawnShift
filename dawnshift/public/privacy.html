Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

The short version: Dawnshift keeps your data on your own device. We, the developer, don't collect it, see it or sell it. The only data that leaves your device goes straight to the AI services, websites and apps that you choose to connect.

What Dawnshift stores on your device

  • Your AI connections, including the API keys you enter;
  • Your app keys (tokens for other apps you connect);
  • Your bots, their settings and the notes they save to memory;
  • Your tasks and the conversations with bots about them;
  • Posts on the team board, and files in the workspace folder.

This data stays on your device: in Dawnshift's "data" folder if you use the downloadable version, or in your browser's storage for dawnshift.dawnlife.online if you use the web version. We have no servers that receive it.

What leaves your device, and where it goes

  • AI services you connect (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, Google, Groq or OpenRouter). When a bot works, Dawnshift sends that service your task, the bot's instructions and memory, and the results of its tools, using your API key. The service handles that data under its own privacy policy. Local models such as Ollama or LM Studio keep everything on your own computer.
  • Websites and apps your bots use. If a bot reads a web page or calls an app you connected, the request goes to that website or app.
  • The Dawnshift website. When you open the web version, your browser downloads the app from the website like any web page, so the website's host sees normal request details such as your IP address. The app never sends your keys, tasks or files there.
  • Google Fonts. The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google can see your IP address when the fonts load. See Google's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't have accounts, and we don't ask for your name, email or location.
  • We don't use analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies.
  • We don't sell or share your data, because we never receive it.

Keeping your data safe

In the downloadable version, your keys and data are saved in a file that only your user account on the device can read. In the web version, they're saved in your browser, where other websites can't read them. Either way, anyone who uses your device (or your browser profile) can open Dawnshift, so protect your device with a password or lock screen.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual bots, tasks, memory notes and keys in the app. To delete everything in the downloadable version, delete its folder, including the "data" folder. In the web version, remove the installed app and clear the Dawnshift website's data in your browser settings. To delete data held by an AI service, contact that service or use its settings.

Children

Dawnshift isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect anyone's personal data. Some AI services have their own age limits. Check them before connecting.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its "Last updated" date. If a change affects how your data is handled, we'll also mention it in the app.
