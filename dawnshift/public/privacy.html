Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

The short version: Dawnshift keeps your data on your own device. If you create an account, an encrypted copy is stored on our server so it can sync between your devices, but it's locked with your password and we can't read it. We don't sell anything, track you or show ads. The only readable data that leaves your device goes straight to the AI services, websites and apps that you choose to connect.

What Dawnshift stores on your device

  • Your AI connections, including the API keys you enter;
  • Your app keys (tokens for other apps you connect);
  • Your bots, their settings and the notes they save to memory;
  • Your tasks and the conversations with bots about them;
  • Posts on the team board, and files in the workspace folder.

This data stays on your device: in the app's data folder if you use the desktop app (File → Open data folder shows it), in Dawnshift's "data" folder if you use the ZIP download, inside the app if you use the Android app, or in your browser's storage for dawnshift.dawnlife.online if you use the web version. Without an account, we have no servers that receive it.

What leaves your device, and where it goes

  • AI services you connect (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, Google, Groq or OpenRouter). When a bot works, Dawnshift sends that service your task, the bot's instructions and memory, and the results of its tools, using your API key. The service handles that data under its own privacy policy. Local models such as Ollama or LM Studio keep everything on your own computer.
  • Websites and apps your bots use. If a bot reads a web page or calls an app you connected, the request goes to that website or app.
  • The Dawnshift website. When you open the web version, your browser downloads the app from the website like any web page, so the website's host sees normal request details such as your IP address. The desktop, ZIP and Android versions also ask the website whether accounts are available when they start and every so often, which shows it your IP address. Without an account, Dawnshift never sends your keys, tasks or files there.
  • Google Fonts. The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google can see your IP address when the fonts load. See Google's privacy policy.

If you create an account

Accounts are optional. They let you use the same team on several devices: the website, the Android app and the desktop app. For each account, our server stores:

  • Your username. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • A scrambled login check. Your password never leaves your device. The device turns it into a one-way code, which the server scrambles again before storing it.
  • Your synced data, encrypted on your device with a key only your password or recovery code can unlock. This includes your AI connections, API keys, app keys, bots, memory, tasks, team board and files. We can't read any of it.
  • Login sessions: a scrambled code for each device you're logged in on, which expires after 60 days.

To slow down password guessing, the server briefly remembers failed login attempts together with the IP address they came from. This is kept in memory for 15 minutes and never saved.

Because we can't read your data, we can't recover it either. If you forget your password and lose your recovery code, your synced data can't be unlocked. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Account → Delete account), which deletes it and your synced data from the server straight away.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email or location.
  • We don't use analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies.
  • We don't sell or share your data. Without an account we never receive it, and with one we only ever hold an encrypted copy we can't read.

Keeping your data safe

In the desktop app and the ZIP download, your keys and data (and, if you're logged in, the key that unlocks your synced data) are saved in files that only your user account on the device can read. In the web version, they're saved in your browser, where other websites can't read them; in the Android app, inside the app. Either way, anyone who uses your device (or your browser profile) can open Dawnshift, so protect your device with a password or lock screen.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual bots, tasks, memory notes and keys in the app. To delete everything in the desktop app, log out and choose to remove your data, or delete its data folder (File → Open data folder); for the ZIP download, delete its folder, including the "data" folder. In the web version, log out and choose to remove your data from the device (or clear the Dawnshift website's data in your browser settings), and delete your account if you have one. To delete data held by an AI service, contact that service or use its settings.

Children

Dawnshift isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect anyone's personal data. Some AI services have their own age limits. Check them before connecting.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its "Last updated" date. If a change affects how your data is handled, we'll also mention it in the app.
