Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

The short version: to use Dawnshift's website or Android app you create an account, and everything in it (your bots, tasks, files and AI keys) is stored on our server so your bots can work for you and your team follows you to every device. It's protected, but it isn't end-to-end encrypted, so the person running the server can technically access it. We only do that when it's needed to run, fix or protect Dawnshift, or when the law requires it. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you. Your content only leaves our server to go to the AI services and apps you choose.

Who is responsible

Dawnshift is run by OliverZS2005 ("we", "us"), who is responsible for your personal data under this policy (the "data controller"). Contact: contact@dawnlife.online.

What we store, and why

  • Your account: your username, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password and of your recovery code, and the date you signed up. We can't see your actual password or recovery code. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number. Why: to give you an account.
  • Login sessions: a hashed code for each device you're logged in on, which expires after 60 days or when you log out. Why: to keep you logged in.
  • Your content: your bots and their settings and memory, tasks and the conversations with bots about them, team board posts, workspace files, AI connections including your API keys, and app keys. Why: this is the service itself; bots need it to work, including while you're offline.
  • Failed logins: to slow down password guessing, the server briefly remembers failed login attempts together with the IP address they came from. This is kept in memory for 15 minutes and never saved to disk. Why: security.
  • Technical logs: like most websites, our web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, date and time, the page or address requested, browser type) and error logs. They're kept for a short time, normally about two weeks, then deleted automatically. Why: to keep the service running, fix problems and stop abuse.

The legal basis for most of this is that it's needed to provide the service you signed up for. Security measures and logs are based on our legitimate interest in keeping Dawnshift safe and working.

Where your data goes

  • AI services you connect (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, Google, Groq or OpenRouter). When a bot works, our server sends that service your instructions, the bot's job and memory, file contents and tool results, using your API key. The service handles that data under its own privacy policy, and many of these services are based in the United States.
  • Websites and apps your bots use. If a bot reads a web page or calls an app you connected, the request is sent from our server to that website or app.
  • Cloudflare sits in front of our server to deliver the website and protect it from attacks, so it handles the traffic between your device and our server, including your IP address.
  • Google Fonts. The app loads its fonts from Google, so Google sees your IP address when the fonts load.
  • GitHub. If you download one of the Dawnshift apps, the download comes from GitHub, which sees your IP address.
  • The law. We may share data when the law requires it, to report serious illegal content such as child sexual abuse material, or to protect someone's safety.

We don't sell your data or share it with anyone for advertising. When data goes to services outside the EU, it's because you chose to connect them, or it's part of how those services (like Cloudflare, Google and GitHub) deliver the website and downloads.

Who can see your data

Your content is only shown to people logged in to your account. On the server it's stored in files that only the Dawnshift service can read. It isn't end-to-end encrypted, so the server's operator can technically access it. We only look at account data when it's needed to keep Dawnshift running, fix a problem you reported, investigate abuse or a security issue, or follow the law.

On your device

We don't use cookies, analytics or tracking. Dawnshift saves a few small things in your browser's local storage: your login token, that you agreed to the terms, whether notifications are on, and whether you closed the iPhone install hint. Notifications are shown by your own browser; we don't use a push notification service. The desktop and ZIP versions, when used without an account, keep all their data in a folder on your own computer and don't send it to us.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and content: until you delete them, or until we delete an account that hasn't been used for 12 months.
  • When you delete your account, its content is removed from the server straight away. If we have backups, copies in them are removed when those backups expire, within 30 days.
  • Logs: normally about two weeks. Failed login attempts: 15 minutes.

Keeping it safe

Everything between your device and our server goes over HTTPS. Passwords and recovery codes are stored only as hashes, and login attempts are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password that you don't use anywhere else, and give Dawnshift separate API keys with spending limits, so you can revoke them on their own if you ever need to. If we learn of a security breach that puts your data at risk, we'll tell affected users in the app and the authorities where the law requires it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU's GDPR), you can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to limit or object to how we use it, or to receive it in a portable format. You can delete individual items and your whole account yourself in the app. For anything else, email contact@dawnlife.online from any address and include your username; we may ask you to prove it's your account, for example by logging in. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Denmark that's Datatilsynet (datatilsynet.dk).

Children

Dawnshift isn't meant for children under 13, and users under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it. Many AI services have their own age limits; check them before connecting.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its "Last updated" date. For important changes we'll also ask you to agree again when you next open Dawnshift.

Contact

Questions about privacy: email contact@dawnlife.online.
