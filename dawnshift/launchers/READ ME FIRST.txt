DAWNSHIFT: your AI team, on your computer
==========================================

HOW TO START

1. Install Node.js (one time only)
   Go to https://nodejs.org, download the "LTS" version and install it.

2. Start Dawnshift
   Windows: double-click "Start Dawnshift (Windows).bat"
   Mac:     double-click "Start Dawnshift (Mac).command"
            The first time, macOS may say it can't check the file. If so,
            right-click the file, choose "Open", then click "Open" again.
   Linux:   run ./start-linux.sh

   A small window opens, and Dawnshift opens in your web browser by itself.
   If it doesn't, go to http://localhost:3777

3. Keep the small window open while you use Dawnshift. Your bots only work
   while it's open. Close it to stop Dawnshift.


CONNECTING AN AI (FREE OPTIONS)

- Gemini: get a free key at https://aistudio.google.com/apikey
- Groq: get a free key at https://console.groq.com/keys
- Ollama or LM Studio: free AI models that run on your own computer

You can also use paid keys from Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT) or
xAI (Grok).


YOUR DATA

Everything, including your API keys, is saved in the "data" folder next to
this file. It never gets sent to us. To move Dawnshift to another computer,
copy the whole folder. To delete everything, delete the whole folder.
